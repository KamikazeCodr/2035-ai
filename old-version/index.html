AI 2035 (Cybersecurity Standpoint)

Footnotes About New Version →
Aditya Singh

I predict that the impact of autonomous AI on cybersecurity and critical infrastructure over the next decade will be severe, fundamentally altering offensive and defensive capabilities.

The critical-infrastructure pathway does not require superintelligence. The decisive capability threshold is reliable autonomous operation across enterprise IT and industrial control networks, allowing offensive capability to scale like parallel cloud software.
